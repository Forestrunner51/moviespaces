HOW IT WORKS

A crew in three taps

No swiping, no profiles to grind through. The movie is the icebreaker.

1

Pick a showing

Real theaters, real times, near you — or name a place for a watch party.

2

Get grouped

You're seated with up to five other people going to the same showing. Small on purpose.

3

Plan it together

Chat opens instantly. Flag your ticket, vote what you're up for after — drinks, food, the spoiler debrief.

WHAT'S INSIDE

Built for going out, not scrolling

Movie Crew

Groups cap at six people who picked the same showing — big enough to feel like a night out, small enough that everyone actually talks.

Spaces

Host a screening or a watch party. Friends or anyone nearby can join, RSVP, split the cost, and chat — all in one place.

Hangout after

Everyone votes one thing they're up for after the credits — so the night doesn't end when the lights come on.

CineMind

A daily movie puzzle with streaks and leaderboards — for you, your friends, and your clubs.
