HOW A NIGHT GOES

One showing. Five strangers. One argument about the ending.

No swiping, no profiles to grind through. The film is the icebreaker.

  1. TUESDAY

    You pick a real showing. Saturday, 7:30, the theater ten minutes from you.

  2. WEDNESDAY

    Two people join. The chat opens itself. Someone asks whether they need to have seen the first one.

  3. SATURDAY · 7:29 PM

    Five of you in the same row. Nobody had to send a single group text.

  4. 9:48 PM

    Credits. Nobody gets up.

  5. 10:15 PM

    The diner across the parking lot. You finally have someone to tell.

WHAT'S INSIDE

Built for talking about films, not scrolling past them

  • Clubs

    A room per genre, full of people who watch what you watch. Say what you just saw and get an actual conversation back, whether or not anyone was sitting next to you.

  • Movie Crew

    Groups cap at six people who picked the same showing. Big enough to feel like a night out, small enough that everyone actually talks.

  • Spaces

    Host a screening or a watch party, at a theater, your place, anywhere with a screen. Friends or anyone nearby can join, RSVP, split the cost, and chat.

  • Hangout

    Everyone votes one thing they're up for after, so the night has somewhere to go instead of ending in the parking lot.

  • CineMind

    A daily movie puzzle with streaks and leaderboards, for you and your friends and whichever club you're in.
