ABOUT

It started with one night in October.

One day in October I walked into a theater not expecting much, and walked out different. The movie itself mattered — but what stayed with me was everything around it: a dark room full of strangers gasping at the same moment, the conversation spilling out into the parking lot, the feeling that for two hours a room of people who'd never met were somewhere together.

I spent a long time trying to recapture that. And I kept running into the same wall — not the movies, the logistics. Getting friends to commit to a showing takes a group chat and a small miracle. Finding people who want to see the same film you do, at the same theater, on the same night? There was no way to do it at all. And when I looked into renting a theater for a private screening, the process was so opaque it felt like it was designed to make you give up.

The best part of the movies was never the screen. It was the room.

So I built the thing I kept wishing existed. An app where you pick a real showing near you and get grouped with a handful of people going to it. Where hosting a movie night — at a theater, at your place, anywhere with a screen — takes a minute instead of a week of planning. Where the night doesn't end at the credits, because everyone's already said what they're up for after.

And thus MovieSpaces was born. We believe movies are a social event — that they're better in a room, with people, with somewhere to go afterwards. If one night at the movies can change your year, imagine what a habit of them can do.

OLAOLUWA KAYODE
Founder, MovieSpaces
