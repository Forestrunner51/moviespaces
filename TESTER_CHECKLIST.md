# MovieSpaces — Beta Test Checklist

Thanks for testing! Work through the flows below on your phone (~20–30 min).
Tap each box as you go. Items marked **[CHECK CLOSELY]** are new or just
changed — please give those extra attention.

**How to report a bug:** tell us **which step**, **what you expected**,
**what actually happened**, and a **screenshot** if you can. "The poster for
_X_ was wrong" beats "posters are broken." No detail is too small.

---

## Before you start
- Install via the TestFlight link we sent (install the TestFlight app first if prompted).
- Open MovieSpaces — check the name, icon, and font look right.
- A **second account / second person** helps for joining a Space, chat, and friend requests. Solo is fine for everything else.
- Allow **location, notifications, and photos** when asked. If you deny one, a couple of features look empty — that's expected.

## A. First launch & signing in
_Try to create an account each way you can — this is what we most need proven on real phones._
- [ ] Sign up with **email + password** → land in the app (or "check your email" to confirm, then sign in)
- [ ] **[CHECK CLOSELY]** Sign in with **Apple** → the dialog appears, you confirm, you land in the app with your name filled in _(report any error code)_
- [ ] **[CHECK CLOSELY]** Sign in with **Google** → a browser opens, you pick your account, it returns and signs you in
- [ ] Sign out, then sign back in with email + password → your data is still there
- [ ] Use **Forgot password** → get a numeric code by email → set a new password → signed in

## B. Your profile
- [ ] Set a **display name** and a **profile photo**
- [ ] In Settings, **change your password** (note the "Forgot your current password?" link)

## C. Create a MovieSpace (a real screening)
- [ ] Start a new Space and **search for a movie** (results appear with posters)
- [ ] **[CHECK CLOSELY]** Confirm the **poster is the RIGHT film** — if any movie shows the wrong poster, tell us the exact title
- [ ] **Pick a showtime** — real nearby theater, date, and time (needs location on)
- [ ] **Create** the Space → it appears with the correct poster, theater, and time

## D. Create a Watch Party / private event
- [ ] Start a **Watch Party** with a custom venue (venue search shows nearby places — theaters, bars, community spaces all count here). Set a cost and capacity.
- [ ] Confirm the venues shown are **near you** _(on a simulator they'll be wherever its location is set)_

## E. Join a Space & RSVP
_Easiest with a second account — have it create a Space first._
- [ ] Find a Space in **Explore** and open it
- [ ] **Join**, then **confirm and cancel** your RSVP (updates immediately both ways)
- [ ] Open a **shared Space link while logged out** → after login it takes you straight there

## F. Group chat
- [ ] **Send and receive** messages in a Space (arrive promptly on both accounts)
- [ ] Open the keyboard and type → it never covers Send, and the screen doesn't flicker

## G. Friends & direct messages
- [ ] **Search a user**, send a friend request, **accept** it on the other account
- [ ] **DM a friend** (you should NOT be able to DM a non-friend)
- [ ] **Block and report** a user → their content disappears for you

## H. CineMind — the daily puzzle
- [ ] Play today's puzzle — all **five** challenges (Connection, Chronos, Cast Deduct, Mystery Movie, Mystery TV)
- [ ] **[CHECK CLOSELY]** On Mystery Movie: a **plot clue shows from the start**, and the difficulty reads **Easy = 3 tries, Medium = 2, Hard = 1** — the "Attempt X of Y" counter should match
- [ ] **Submit** → you get a score, your streak, and a shareable result
- [ ] Try to play again the same day → it's **locked** ("already played")

## I. Movie Roulette — practice
- [ ] **Spin** a few times and play the practice challenges (says practice doesn't affect your streak)
- [ ] **[CHECK CLOSELY]** Across several spins, the wrong-answer **options feel varied**, not the same few names repeating

## J. Notifications
- [ ] With notifications allowed, trigger a few (new message, RSVP, booking, reminder) → they actually arrive

## K. Host controls (on a Space you created)
- [ ] **Mark as booked**, and **hand off ownership** to someone (they get host controls)
- [ ] **Add to Calendar** (right details) and **Get Tickets** (opens the booking page)
- [ ] **Cancel** and **delete** a Space (attendees notified; deleted Space is gone)
- [ ] Open a Space whose showtime has **passed** → it locks to chat-only

## L. Account
- [ ] **Delete your account** (use a throwaway one!) → account and data actually removed, and you're signed out

## M. Anything that feels off
- [ ] Note any **crash, freeze, or blank screen** (what were you doing right before?)
- [ ] Note **wrong posters, odd wording, or text running off the screen** (screenshots help)
- [ ] Note anything **slow or confusing** — where you got stuck is gold
